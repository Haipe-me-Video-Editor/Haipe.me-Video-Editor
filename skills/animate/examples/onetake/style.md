# Style bible: A reel, from one dot, in one take

## Palette
- night `#1e1e28` background, flat (kept above black: qc fails a near-black frame).
- dot / record `#ff5a36` (the accent; the one warm thing on screen).
- screen `#2a2a36`, rim `#4a4a5e` for the phone, track `#353542`.
- takes: `#ff5a36`, `#ffb347`, `#4fb3a9`, `#7b8cff`.
- words `#f3ede2`.

## Shape and line
Flat shapes with round corners, no outlines except the phone's thin rim. Every shape on screen is grown from the one
before it: the dot is the record button, the button is the phone, the phone holds the takes, the takes are the bars,
the bars fold back into the dot.

## Texture and light
A soft glow behind the accent only, light grain, a faint vignette.

## Motion
Springy grows, quick slides with an ease-out, bars that hit on the beat and fall back. Nothing pops in from nowhere and
nothing cuts: one camera over the whole film, a slow push in and a small tilt on the beat.

## Words on screen
Instrument Serif Italic, 84 px, one short line, upper third, fading in after each beat starts and out before it ends.

## Never
A hard cut, a dissolve, a shape that appears from nothing, more than one line of words.
